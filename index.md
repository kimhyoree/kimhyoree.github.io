## Welcome!
I am Hyoree Kim, a researcher at the **Seoul National University Hospital, Department of Radiology, Biomedical Intelligence Laboratory (BiLab)**, Seoul, Republic of Korea. I am passionate about bridging **artificial intelligence** and **medicine**, building intelligent systems that assist clinicians and improve patient outcomes.
My research spans **radiology AI**, **computational pathology**, and **computer vision**, with hands-on experience in medical image analysis, whole slide image (WSI) analysis, transfer learning with pathology foundation models, and multimodal learning.
At BiLab, I work on **medical imaging AI** and **vision-language models** for radiology, with a focus on building systems that are evidence-grounded, verifiable, and clinically useful.
## Research Interests
- **Radiology AI:** Medical Image Analysis, Multimodal Learning, Diagnostic Assistance Algorithms
- **Computational Pathology:** Whole Slide Image Analysis, IHC Tissue Segmentation, Foundation Model Transfer Learning
- **Computer Vision:** Segmentation, Multimodal Learning, Foundation Models
