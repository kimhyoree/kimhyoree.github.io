## Welcome!

I am Hyoree Kim, a researcher at the **Biomedical Intelligence Laboratory
(BiLab), Department of Radiology, Seoul National University Hospital.** I received my B.S. from the **School of AI Convergence** at **Sungshin Women's University**, Seoul, Korea. I am passionate about bridging **artificial intelligence** and **medicine**, building intelligent systems that assist clinicians and improve patient outcomes.

My research spans **medical image analysis**, **computational pathology**, and **computer vision**, with hands-on experience in whole slide image (WSI) analysis, transfer learning with pathology foundation models, and multimodal learning. Previously, I worked as a research intern at **Seoul National University Hospital (BMI Lab)** and the **Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST)**.

I am currently seeking opportunities for graduate studies in **Medical AI / Medical Informatics**, where I hope to continue developing AI solutions that make a real-world clinical impact.

## Research Interests

- **Radiology AI:** Medical Image Analysis, Multimodal Learning, Diagnostic Assistance Algorithms
- **Computational Pathology:** IHC Tissue Segmentation, Toxicologic Pathology Screening, Foundation Model Transfer Learning
- **Computer Vision:** Whole Slide Image (WSI) Analysis, Segmentation, Foundation Models
